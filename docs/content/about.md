Welcome to Riverbank Bingo, Kahnawake's premier live-streamed bingo experience!
Driven by a passion for community, excitement and innovation, we bring the
classic thrill of the bingo hall straight to the comfort of your living room.

We have redefined how our bingo community plays, wins and connects. No crowded
rooms or long commutes — just pure fun, broadcast live to your favourite screen.

## The Riverbank Experience

Every second Tuesday evening at 6:45 PM (Eastern), our live YouTube broadcast
transforms living rooms across the Greater Montreal Area (and beyond!) into
interactive gaming hubs. We design our streams to be accessible and easy to
follow, with colour-coded visual cues that match your cards perfectly. When you
hit that winning pattern, our dedicated hotline (450-632-4646) is open and
waiting for your call to verify your win live on air!

## Proudly Local, Broadly Reaching

While our roots are deeply planted in Kahnawake, our bingo community spans far
and wide. We make playing effortless:

- **Local pickups:** Grab your physical cards at trusted community distributors.
- **To your door:** For players in extended regions — including Île-Perrot,
  Coteau-du-Lac and parts of New York — we proudly offer convenient, contactless
  card delivery.

## Nice Pots, Fast Payouts

We believe good fun deserves nice rewards. That's why Riverbank Bingo features
highly competitive prize pools, including three regular $1,000 jackpots and
progressive specials that keep on growing. Best of all, our winners are
celebrated instantly, and your winnings are sent straight to your bank account
by secure e-Transfer within 24–48 hours — quite often the very same night.

## Join the Riverbank Family!

Whether you're a seasoned player chasing the next progressive jackpot or looking
for a fun Tuesday night in with family, there's a seat at our virtual table for
you. Get your cards ready, tune in to our channel, and get ready to shout BINGO!
